# MARSWALK · 30-second video script (English)

> Story: an astronaut lands on Mars and doesn't know which way to go (**problem**) → MARSWALK shows the safe path (**solution**) → what it could do next (**future**).
> Follows the official structure of the **2026 Project Submission Guide** (see the checklist at the end).
> Screen recordings: shots A–E from [guion.md §3](guion.md) (Vercel version, 1080p). Stills already exist in `capturas/`.
> **No logo:** the project name appears as large text only.

## Checklist from the official 2026 rules

Source: [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) (Nov 13, 2026).

- [ ] **30 seconds or less.** Projects over the limit are *not eligible for Global Judging*. Export at 29–30 s, never 31.
- [ ] **English subtitles** burned into the video (or the SRT above uploaded to YouTube).
- [ ] **Structure:** 0:00 team · 0:02 challenge · 0:05 name + tagline · 0:10 how it works + NASA data · 0:20–0:30 the future. ✓ covered above.
- [ ] **Public link** with no login (YouTube unlisted, Drive "anyone with the link", etc.). Videos cannot be uploaded to the Space Apps site.
- [ ] **No NASA branding:** no NASA logo, flag or mission patches in the video. Plain text like "NASA HiRISE data" is fine.
- [ ] **AI-generated images or video** (for example, the astronaut) must carry a **visible watermark** saying they were AI-generated, and the README line *"no images were generated with AI"* must be updated.
- [ ] **No people under 18** in images, video or audio.
- [ ] Music and images: use only ones you have permission for (royalty-free music; Curiosity photo credited NASA/JPL-Caltech).
- [ ] Submit with **"Submit for Judging"** before **Sunday, Nov 15, 11:59 PM** local time.
